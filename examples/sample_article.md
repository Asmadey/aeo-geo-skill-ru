# Modern Enterprise Search Architecture

In recent years, enterprise search has changed quite a bit. Many companies are wondering how they should modernize their knowledge systems.

There are many options available. Some organizations use Elasticsearch while others are exploring vector databases. We think vector search is generally pretty fast and helpful for engineers.

In our internal tests, vector databases reduced query latency significantly. It is important to structure your documents properly and provide good metadata.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What is vector search?
Vector search uses mathematical embeddings to match concepts rather than exact keywords.

How do I start?
You should review your current data pipeline and pick a vector database.
