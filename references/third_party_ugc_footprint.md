# Third-Party & UGC Footprint: Engineering the 85% Earned Moat

A fundamental law of Generative Engine Optimization: **AI models do not take your word for it.**

While your owned domain establishes official messaging and machine-readable data, **85% of brand recommendations and entity co-occurrences in AI answers originate from third-party websites.**

---

## 1. The Evidence: Mentions vs. Backlinks

- **Brand Mentions Correlation with AI Overviews:** **r = 0.664** (Strong predictor)
- **Traditional Backlink Domain Authority (DA) Correlation:** **r = 0.218** (Weak predictor)
- **UGC Platform Dominance:** Reddit, YouTube, Quora, and LinkedIn account for **17.1% of all cited domains in AI answers** — representing 4× the volume of traditional digital publishers.

AI engines perform **cross-document consensus validation**. If your site claims "We are the most reliable tool for X", but Reddit discussions, customer reviews, and industry analysts mention your competitor, the AI engine will cite your competitor.

---

## 2. Platform Playbooks

### A. Reddit: Authentic Problem-Solving & Entity Association
* **Why it works:** Google has direct partnership data access to Reddit. Perplexity and ChatGPT heavily scrape Reddit for unvarnished consumer consensus.
* **Selection Criteria:** Identify 3–5 core subreddits where:
  1. The audience actively debates tooling / purchasing decisions.
  2. Moderation is active and spam is filtered.
  3. You can provide verifiable answers without direct commercial promotion.
* **Ethical Rules:**
  - Always disclose affiliation: *"I work on [Brand] engineering team..."*
  - Answer the technical question completely inside the comment. Never post bare marketing links.
  - Case Study (Kumon): Systematic, value-first Reddit participation over 5 months grew brand visibility in ChatGPT parent queries from **5% to 18%**.
* **What NEVER to do:** Do not purchase aged Reddit accounts or deploy automated AI response bots. Reddit's anti-spam models blocked 23M+ spam views in 2025; domains linked in banned spam threads suffer cross-engine devaluation.

### B. YouTube: Long-Form Technical Proof
* **94% of AI-cited videos are Long-Form (10+ minutes).** AI search models transcribe and ingest video audio to verify procedural steps.
* **40% of cited videos have fewer than 1,000 views.** Unlike the YouTube recommendation algorithm (which optimizes for clicks and watch time), AI search engines optimize for **semantic match and topical depth**. A 12-minute technical walkthrough with 400 views regularly out-cites a viral influencer video.
* **Requirements:**
  - Clear, audible spoken keywords and terminology.
  - Step-by-step screen recordings with visible UI labels.
  - Structured video chapters and timestamps in the description.

### C. Industry Software Aggregators (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot)
* When users prompt: *"Compare the top 5 enterprise CRM solutions for biotech"*, AI engines consult aggregator category taxonomies.
* If your product is not listed in the correct category taxonomy on Gartner/G2/Capterra, you are mathematically excluded from the model's consideration set.
* Ensure feature checklists, pricing disclosures, and customer verified reviews are refreshed quarterly.

---

## 3. The Co-Occurrence Engine

AI models represent brand reputation through **word vector co-occurrences**.

To become the default recommendation in your niche, ensure your brand name frequently appears in the same paragraph as:
1. Your target category keyword (e.g., `vector search`, `Kubernetes observability`).
2. High-value problem statements (e.g., `reduced egress fees`, `zero downtime migrations`).
3. Recognized industry peers in fair comparison contexts (`Brand X vs Competitor Y`).

### What to Avoid:
- **PBNs & Cheap Sponsored Posts:** Modern LLMs detect boilerplate sponsored layouts and downweight low-signal link farms.
- **Manufactured Reviews:** LLMs cross-reference review sentiment across multiple independent platforms. Outlier positive clusters with low account history are discounted.
